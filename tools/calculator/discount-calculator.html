💰 What is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction in the original price of a product or service. It is typically expressed as a percentage (%) of the original price. Discounts are offered by retailers and brands to attract customers, clear old inventory, reward loyalty, or boost sales during festive seasons.

  • Seasonal / Festive Discounts: Offered during holidays, Diwali, Black Friday, New Year, etc.
  • Clearance / End-of-Season Discounts: To sell off remaining stock before new collections arrive.
  • Volume / Bulk Discounts: Reduced prices when you buy in large quantities.
  • Membership / Loyalty Discounts: Exclusive deals for members or repeat customers.
  • Cashback / Promo Codes: Indirect discounts where you get money back or a code gives you a percentage off.

Understanding how discounts work is essential for budget-conscious shoppers. A 50% discount on a ₹10,000 item saves you ₹5,000, while the same percentage on a ₹1,000 item saves only ₹500. This is why calculating the actual discount amount in rupees is more meaningful than just looking at the percentage.

📐 Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % / 100) Price After Discount = Original Price – Discount Amount Tax Amount = Price After Discount × (Tax % / 100) Final Price = Price After Discount + Tax Amount You Save = Discount Amount

🧮 Example Calculation

Scenario: You want to buy a pair of premium headphones originally priced at ₹8,999. The store offers a 22% discount. Additionally, a 12% GST is applicable on the discounted price.

Discount Amount: ₹8,999 × 0.22 = ₹1,979.78

Price After Discount: ₹8,999 – ₹1,979.78 = ₹7,019.22

Tax Amount: ₹7,019.22 × 0.12 = ₹842.31

Final Price: ₹7,019.22 + ₹842.31 = ₹7,861.53

You Save: ₹1,979.78

Result: You pay ₹7,861.53 instead of ₹8,999, saving ₹1,979.78.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the difference between discount and tax?
Discount is a reduction applied to the original price. Tax is an additional charge levied by the government. Tax is calculated on the discounted price, not the original price.
Can I use this calculator for any currency?
Absolutely! The formulas work with any currency. Simply treat the input as your local currency.
Why is tax calculated on the discounted price?
In most tax systems (including GST in India), tax is levied on the transaction value – the price actually paid after discounts.
Is the calculator accurate for decimal percentages?
Yes! You can enter discount and tax rates with decimals (e.g., 22.5%, 7.75%). The calculator handles floating-point arithmetic with precision.
Can I calculate multiple discounts at once?
The current version applies a single discount and a single tax. For multiple discounts, apply them sequentially.
