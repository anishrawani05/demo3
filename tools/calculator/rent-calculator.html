🧮 Formula Math

The 99toolhub Rent Calculator uses a transparent and logical formula to compute your total rental cost. Understanding the underlying mathematics helps you trust the results and even perform manual calculations if needed. Here's the complete breakdown:

1. Monthly Total = Base Rent + Σ(Additional Charges)
Where Additional Charges include maintenance, electricity, water, gas, internet, parking, society fees, cleaning, and other miscellaneous costs.
2. Total Rent Over Period = Σ(Monthly Total × (1 + Annual Increase)Year)
The annual increase is applied to the monthly total at the end of each 12-month period, compounding over the lease duration.
3. Discount Amount = (Discount Type === 'Percentage') ? Total Rent × (Discount % / 100) : Fixed Discount
You can choose between a fixed discount (e.g., ₹5,000 off) or a percentage discount (e.g., 10% off the total rent).
4. Grand Total = Total Rent – Discount Amount
This is the final amount you will pay over the entire lease period, excluding the security deposit (which is refundable).
5. Average Monthly Cost = Grand Total / Number of Months
This gives you the effective monthly cost after accounting for discounts and annual increases. It's a useful metric for comparing different rental options.

The Yearly Cost is simply the average monthly cost multiplied by 12, giving you an annualized figure for budgeting purposes. The Savings displayed is the total discount amount you received, which represents the money you saved through negotiation or special offers.

📌 Example Calculation Demo

Let's walk through a detailed example to see how the 99toolhub Rent Calculator works in practice. Suppose you are considering a rental property with the following details:

Monthly Rent: ₹15,000
Lease Duration: 12 Months
Security Deposit: ₹30,000
Maintenance: ₹2,000/month
Electricity: ₹1,500/month
Water: ₹500/month
Internet: ₹700/month
Parking: ₹600/month
Society Fees: ₹400/month
Annual Increase: 5%
Discount: ₹5,000 (Fixed)

Step 1: Calculate Monthly Total
Monthly Total = Base Rent + All Additional Charges
= ₹15,000 + (₹2,000 + ₹1,500 + ₹500 + ₹700 + ₹600 + ₹400)
= ₹15,000 + ₹5,700 = ₹20,700

Step 2: Calculate Total Rent Over 12 Months
Since the lease is 12 months, the annual increase of 5% is applied after the first year. For a 12-month lease, no increase is applied (as it's exactly 1 year).
Total Rent = ₹20,700 × 12 = ₹2,48,400

Step 3: Apply Discount
Discount = ₹5,000 (Fixed)
Grand Total = ₹2,48,400 – ₹5,000 = ₹2,43,400

Step 4: Average Monthly Cost
Average Monthly = ₹2,43,400 / 12 = ₹20,283

Step 5: Security Deposit
Security Deposit = ₹30,000 (refundable at the end of the lease)

Result Summary:
• Total Rent: ₹2,48,400
• Discount: ₹5,000
• Grand Total: ₹2,43,400
• Average Monthly Cost: ₹20,283
• Security Deposit: ₹30,000
• Total Upfront Cost: ₹2,73,400 (Grand Total + Deposit)

This example demonstrates how the calculator accounts for all variables to give you a complete picture of your rental commitment. You can adjust any parameter — such as the discount type, annual increase percentage, or additional charges — to see how it affects the final total.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

How is rent calculated?
Rent is calculated by taking the base monthly rent, adding all additional charges (maintenance, electricity, water, internet, etc.), applying any annual increase (compounded over the lease period), and then subtracting any discounts. The result is the total amount payable over the entire lease term.
Is the security deposit refundable?
Yes, the security deposit is typically refundable at the end of the lease term, subject to the terms of the rental agreement. It is usually returned within 15–30 days after the tenant vacates, minus any deductions for damages or outstanding dues.
How is the annual increase applied?
The annual increase is applied to the monthly total (rent + additional charges) at the end of each 12-month period. For example, if the lease is for 24 months and the increase is 5%, the monthly total for months 13–24 would be 5% higher than months 1–12. This compounds over the lease duration.
What is the difference between fixed and percentage discount?
A fixed discount is a specific amount subtracted from the total rent (e.g., ₹5,000 off). A percentage discount subtracts a percentage of the total rent (e.g., 10% off). Percentage discounts scale with the total amount, while fixed discounts are constant regardless of the total.
Can I download the report?
Yes, you can download the calculation results as a PDF report using the "PDF" button. You can also copy the results to your clipboard, print the page, or share the result via the native share dialog or clipboard.
What currencies are supported?
The 99toolhub Rent Calculator supports Indian Rupee (₹) and US Dollar ($) with live two-way conversion. You can toggle between the two currencies using the buttons at the top of the input section. All values convert instantly using the current exchange rate.
How is the average monthly cost calculated?
The average monthly cost is the grand total (total rent minus discount) divided by the number of months in the lease. This gives you the effective monthly cost, accounting for all additional charges, increases, and discounts.
Is my data saved or shared?
Your data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It is not sent to any server or shared with any third party. You can clear your history at any time using the "Clear" button in the history section.

Absolutely. The 99toolhub Rent Calculator works for both residential and commercial properties. Simply enter the relevant rent, additional charges, and lease terms to get an accurate breakdown of your commercial rental costs.
How do I reset all fields?

How does the live currency conversion work?
