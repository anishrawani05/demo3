🧮 Formula Math

The mathematics behind currency conversion is straightforward but essential to understand. The conversion formula is based on the exchange rate between two currencies:

Converted Amount = Amount × (Exchange Rate of Target Currency / Exchange Rate of Source Currency)
Where the exchange rate is defined as the value of one unit of the source currency expressed in terms of the target currency (or vice versa, depending on the quote convention).

In practice, the tool first converts the input amount from the source currency to a base currency (typically USD) and then from the base currency to the target currency. This two-step process ensures accuracy even when the direct pair is not available. The formula can be expressed as:

Result = Amount × (RateTarget/Base / RateSource/Base)
Where RateTarget/Base is the exchange rate of the target currency relative to the base currency (USD), and RateSource/Base is the exchange rate of the source currency relative to the base currency.

For example, if you want to convert 100 EUR to INR, and the rates are: 1 EUR = 1.08 USD and 1 USD = 83.50 INR, then the conversion would be: 100 × (83.50 / 1.08) = 7,731.48 INR. This is exactly how the calculator works internally, ensuring precision to the last decimal.
