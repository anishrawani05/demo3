📷 What is Image to PDF?

Image to PDF conversion is the process of taking one or more image files and combining them into a single Portable Document Format (PDF) file. PDF is a widely used file format that preserves the layout, fonts, and images exactly as intended, making it ideal for sharing, printing, and archiving.

Unlike JPEG or PNG, which are raster formats, PDFs can contain multiple pages, vector graphics, and text, making them more versatile for documents. Converting images to PDF is especially useful when you need to:

  • Combine multiple scanned documents into one file
  • Create a photo album or portfolio
  • Compile receipts, invoices, or business documents
  • Prepare presentations or reports
  • Convert digital artwork for printing

📊 PDF Quality Explained

The quality of your PDF depends on several factors:

  • Image Quality: Low, Medium, or High — affects the resolution of embedded images. Higher quality = larger file size.
  • Compression: None, Low, Medium, or High — reduces file size by compressing images. Higher compression = smaller file but lower quality.
  • Image Fit: Fit to Page, Fill Page, Original Size, or Stretch — determines how images are scaled on each page.
  • Page Size: A4, A3, A5, Letter, Legal, or Auto — affects the page dimensions and image scaling.

For the best balance of quality and size, we recommend Medium Quality and Medium Compression.

📦 Image Compression Explained

Compression reduces the file size of your PDF by applying algorithms to the embedded images. Our tool offers four levels:

  • None: No compression — maximum quality, largest file size.
  • Low: Light compression — good quality, slightly smaller file.
  • Medium: Balanced compression — recommended for most uses.
  • High: Maximum compression — smallest file, may reduce image quality.

For documents shared via email or stored in the cloud, Medium compression is ideal. For high-quality printing, use None or Low.

📐 Page Size Guide (A4, Letter, Legal)

  • A4: 210 × 297 mm — The most common international standard for documents.
  • A3: 297 × 420 mm — Twice the size of A4, used for posters and diagrams.
  • A5: 148 × 210 mm — Half of A4, used for booklets and flyers.
  • Letter: 216 × 279 mm — The standard US paper size.
  • Legal: 216 × 356 mm — Used for legal documents in the US.
  • Auto: Automatically adjusts the page size to fit each image individually.

🔄 Portrait vs Landscape

Portrait: The page is taller than it is wide. This is the default for most documents, letters, and reports. Images are aligned vertically.

Landscape: The page is wider than it is tall. Ideal for images that are naturally wide, such as panoramas, charts, and presentations.

Choose the orientation that best matches your images. If your images are a mix, you can choose Auto to let the tool decide based on each image's aspect ratio.
